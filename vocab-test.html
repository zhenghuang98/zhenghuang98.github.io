Vocabulary Level Test

Discover your English vocabulary size

This test estimates your English vocabulary size using the top 17,600 most frequent English words, ranked by usage frequency. The test uses an adaptive binary search algorithm to efficiently find your vocabulary boundary.

  • Quick test: ~40–60 words (3–5 minutes)
  • Advanced refinement: ~40 more focused words after quick test
  • Adaptive difficulty that adjusts to your level
  • Detailed results with CEFR level and confidence intervals

For each word, answer whether you know its meaning well enough to use it. If you only vaguely recognize it, choose "Not Sure."
